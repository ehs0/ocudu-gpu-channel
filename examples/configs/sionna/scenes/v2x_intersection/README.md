# v2x_intersection — the V2X blind-intersection scene

Generated by `build_v2x_intersection.py` (plain Python, no Sionna) from
[`examples/v2x_intersection/layout.py`](../../../../v2x_intersection/layout.py),
the module the SUMO network, the vehicle controller and the local
line-of-sight sensor also read. Used by the scenarios in
`examples/configs/sionna/scenarios/v2x_intersection/` (written by the same
script) and by [`examples/v2x_intersection/`](../../../../v2x_intersection/README.md).

```
python3 build_v2x_intersection.py   # scene.xml + meshes/ + manifest.json, and both scenario JSONs
```

| Object | Geometry | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ground` | 400 × 400 m slab at z = 0, intersection centre at the origin | concrete |
| `building_block_{ne,nw,sw,se}` | 62 × 62 × 18 m blocks, faces 8 m from the road centre lines (x, y ∈ ±[8, 70] m) | concrete |

Frame: x east, y north, z up, metres — the same frame as the SUMO network
(built without normalisation), so the bridge runs with a zero position offset.

| Scenario | gNB (m) | Parked `ue1` (m) | Vehicle A (`ue0`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `v2x-intersection-los.json` | (−5, −160, 10), on A's street axis | (−5, −130, 1.5) | line of sight for the whole approach |
| `v2x-intersection-blocked.json` | (−40, −140, 10), south-west | (−40, −110, 1.5) | line of sight until ~49 m before the conflict point, then shadowed by the SW block |

The solver traces LOS, specular reflection and diffraction (depth 3); one
update takes 50–80 ms on the GB10, so the bridge keeps up with a 10 Hz
position feed. With the bridge's default `--gain-offset-db 60`, A's strongest
downlink tap is about −13…−23 dB on the `los` site and falls from about −15 dB
to −30…−35 dB on the `blocked` site
(`examples/v2x_intersection/channel_profile.py`).
